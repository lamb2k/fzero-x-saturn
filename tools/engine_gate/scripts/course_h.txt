# TIMING ONLY (modes-7, not a gate row): cold boot, choose course COURSE (probe 0x2nn), race it in driver mode with the window held at drive
# tick 3780 like course_pr.txt, so a build without Practice (ENG_MODES=0) is scored over the same 420..3780 window.
until 1 1 2000
mark title
wait 220
pad 0800 2
until 2 1 600
wait 4
pad 0040 3
wait 6
poke _fzx_scn_probe 0x2COURSE
wait 3
mark select
pad 0400 2
until 4 1 4000
mark race_start
poke _fzx_e0_hold_tick 3780
untilword _fzx_input_count 0 3780 12000
wait 8
mark results
poke _fzx_e0_hold_tick 0
