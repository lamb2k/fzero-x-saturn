# Control (ctl-pr-solo): the same script as course_pr.txt but it enters Time Attack, Standard (probe 0x411), which races alone.
# course probe would reset a mode that lists fewer courses), confirm through LOAD, race the player in driver mode against the full Practice field
# (29 opponents) to an explicit end-of-window mark at drive tick 3780 (Practice has no finish), then leave through the pause menu (START, DOWN,
# DOWN, A = QUIT) and mark the exit. The gate asserts the mode, class, course, machine, skin, slider bits, field and start grid at race_start
# (engine_gate.py practice_fixture), scores the tick-420 window up to `results`, and checks that QUIT wrote no race record.
# engine_gate.py substitutes COURSE (two hex digits).
until 1 1 2000
mark title
wait 220
pad 0800 2
until 2 1 600
wait 4
pad 0040 3
wait 6
poke _fzx_scn_probe 0x411
wait 3
poke _fzx_scn_probe 0x2COURSE
wait 3
mark select
pad 0400 2
until 4 1 4000
mark race_start
poke _fzx_e0_hold_tick 3780
untilword _fzx_input_count 0 3780 12000
wait 8
mark results
poke _fzx_e0_hold_tick 0
wait 4
pad 0800 2
wait 6
pad 2000 2
wait 4
pad 2000 2
wait 4
pad 0400 2
until 5 1 1200
wait 6
mark quit
