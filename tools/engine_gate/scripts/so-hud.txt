# Cold boot, choose course COURSE in the select (probe 0x2nn), race it in driver mode to RESULTS.
# P3 (codex/p3-frontend): the new TITLE takes Start after its intro (wait 220); X at SELECT sets the engine
# balance to 0.0, the setting of the HOST references.
# engine_gate.py substitutes COURSE (two hex digits).
until 1 1 2000
mark title
wait 220
pad 0800 2
until 2 1 600
wait 4
pad 0040 3
wait 6
poke _fzx_scn_probe 0x2COURSE
wait 3
mark select
pad 0400 2
until 4 1 4000
clip on
wait 1000
clip off
mark race_start
until 5 1 14000
wait 4
mark results
